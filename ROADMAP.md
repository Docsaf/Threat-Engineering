# Roadmap
Artifacts land here after they are proven in a live environment and sanitized. In build order:
1. **SOC POC readout** — the one-page proof-of-concept for an AI-enhanced SOC: three agents, HITL gates, RBAC model, 30-day pilot design and what the pilot actually showed.
2. **Triage doctrine** — severity definitions, escalation clocks, and how inherited thresholds get reconciled with an IR playbook.
3. **RBAC for a two-person security team** — least privilege, separation of duties, PIM-eligible elevation, and sealed break-glass that opens a case on use.
4. **Reporting engines** — the Daily Threat Report and Weekly Threat Brief + Incident Retro templates, and the "no weeks missed, no rework" operating measure.
5. **Threat intelligence on a budget** — wiring CISA KEV, AIS, and community feeds into a SIEM with a quarterly kept-or-cut review.
6. **The evidence pipeline** — collecting audit evidence once and projecting it to every framework a customer asks about.
7. **Cloud baseline pulls as auditable artifacts** — procedure, evidence record, and sealed outputs as one chain of custody.
