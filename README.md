## The thesis
Threat engineering is the discipline between security architecture and security operations: building the detection, response, evidence, and governance machinery so a SOC runs as a system rather than a heroic individual. Every artifact here was proven in a real environment first, then sanitized for publication. Nothing here is theoretical.

The AI dimension is treated with the seriousness it now demands. This portfolio's north star for agent governance is Camille Stewart Gloster's *The Insider You Built: How Organizations Stay in Control of Autonomous AI Agents* (Wiley, 2026) and its Agent-Centered Enforcement and Attribution (ACE) Framework: the reframing of AI incidents as governance challenges, the focus on delegated authority, and the demand for continuous operational control across human and machine decision paths. Our SOC agents are designed so those questions are answerable on day one, not reconstructed after an incident.

## The rules of this repo
1. Proven first, published second. Every artifact ran somewhere real.
2. Sanitized always. Roles, not names; patterns, not tenants; methods, not client details.
3. Sources credited, never reproduced. We point to the authoritative source and add our practice.
4. Humans decide, agents draft. The portfolio practices the governance it preaches.
